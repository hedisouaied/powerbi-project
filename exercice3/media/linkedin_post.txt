🚀 I built a full "Mission Control" business intelligence terminal… inside ONE Power BI page.

No stack of native charts and slicers. The whole experience is a single custom visual I wrote in TypeScript, sitting on a clean semantic model.

🎥 In the video (3 min), I walk through it live: switching from dark to light mode, searching with Ctrl+K, creating a task, asking the assistant questions, running what-if and crisis simulations, replaying history, and rotating a 3D model of the company.

What it does:
🎛️ Command Center: KPI cards with sparklines, business health gauge, a drag-and-drop dashboard builder
🌗 Dark and light themes in one click, saved inside the report
✅ Operations: Kanban task manager, Gantt projects, approvals, a calendar with conflict detection, decision tracking
🔎 Intelligence: anomaly detection, root-cause analysis, network graph, forecasts with backtested error bands
🧪 Simulation: what-if levers, crisis simulator, time machine replay, business sandbox
🏙️ A 3D digital twin of the company and a geo view of Tunisian regions
🗂️ A data explorer and a "Data Doctor" that scores data quality with a transparent formula

Under the hood:
• 31 tables modeled in Power Query + DAX (62 measures, star schema, no ambiguous relationships)
• Every KPI computed in TypeScript is verified against DAX: 46/46 match
• 43 automated UI tests, accessibility and reduced-motion support

What I care about most is honesty in the UI. The AI assistant is rule-based and says so. Every answer cites its data. Simulations are labelled as estimates. Findings say "requires review", never more than the data proves.

All 29 planned features are delivered. The data is synthetic, built for the exercise.
